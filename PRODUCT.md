# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Saudi institutional leaders, owners of strategies without execution capacity, and organizations building ventures or transforming operations. This audience is explicitly described in the original HTML capabilities section.

## Product Purpose

Masbak builds operating entities and stays until they can work independently. The experimental copy presents its Build, Operate, Transfer approach and invites a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Confident, structured, grounded. Preserve the existing Saudi brand, logo and Arabic language; use the user's supplied executive minimalism direction.

## Anti-references

No SaaS template, crypto imagery, neon, fabricated metrics or office claims.

## Design Principles

Execution is the promise. Make the sequence understandable. Preserve source content. Use space and typography to communicate confidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic RTL first, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion and intentional 320px layouts as requested.
